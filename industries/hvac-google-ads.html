What we review

Make HVAC search more relevant

01

Repair versus installation intent

Separate urgent no-heat or no-cooling searches from maintenance and replacement planning. Their landing pages, customer needs and lead economics differ.

02

Real dispatch geography

Target the postal codes, cities or radius your crew actually serves and review location settings and search terms for waste.

03

Emergency service claims

Only advertise emergency availability, turnaround times and opening hours that the HVAC company can genuinely meet.

04

Calls and forms

Make phone actions clear on mobile, route after-hours requests appropriately and test that forms reach someone responsible.

05

Negative keywords and fit

Review queries for employment, DIY instructions, parts-only searches and services the contractor does not offer.

06

Qualified bookings

Distinguish a tracked call from a suitable appointment, estimate, completed job and newly acquired customer.

Before investing

Built for real HVAC operations

  • Residential or commercial HVAC teams with genuine installation, repair or maintenance capacity and defined service territories in the United States or Canada.
  • Operators able to explain which jobs they want more of, their busiest seasons and how dispatch handles incoming enquiries.
  • Firms that can review call disposition, accepted jobs and customer outcomes to inform which keywords and service areas deserve spend.

Budget and scope

HVAC cost per click can vary sharply by location, season and emergency intent. Instead of promising a cheap cost per lead, we assess average job value, conversion rates, dispatch capacity, competition and a testing budget your business can sustain.

Campaign accounts and customer data remain under the client's control, with appropriate permissions and agreed reporting responsibilities.

Questions to ask

What to clarify first

Should repair and installation use the same campaign?

They often have different intent and economics. We assess search demand, budget and conversion volume before deciding how much to separate them.

Can you advertise for towns outside our base?

Only locations the HVAC company genuinely serves. Campaign areas should match technician availability and landing-page information.

Do you offer emergency HVAC lead guarantees?

No. Demand, local competition, budget, responsiveness and seasonal conditions affect enquiry and booking volume.

Does Local Services Ads belong in the plan?

Eligibility and availability depend on Google's current requirements and location. We first check which products the business can use and avoid claiming verification or certification without evidence.
