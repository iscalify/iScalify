How Much Should a Small Business Spend on Meta Ads in India? (2026 Guide)

It's the first question every business owner asks us, and the most honest answer is: it depends on what a customer is worth to you. But "it depends" is a useless answer, so this guide gives you real numbers — the budget ranges we see working for Indian small businesses right now, and what each level actually buys you.

The short answer

For most Indian small businesses in 2026, a starting Meta Ads budget of ₹30,000–₹50,000 per month gives you enough data to know what's working. Below ₹20,000/month, you're usually spending on learning rather than results — the algorithm never exits the learning phase, and you can't tell whether a bad week is a bad ad or just bad luck.

What each budget level buys you

₹20,000–₹30,000/month — Testing budget

This is the minimum to run properly. You can test one audience, 3–4 creatives, and one offer. Expect learning, not scale. Good for: validating whether Meta Ads works for your business at all.

₹50,000–₹1,00,000/month — Growth budget

This is where most of our clients live. You can run separate prospecting and retargeting campaigns, test new creatives weekly, and get statistically meaningful results within 3–4 weeks. This is the sweet spot for D2C brands, clinics, coaching businesses and local services.

₹1,00,000–₹5,00,000/month — Scale budget

Multiple campaigns, multiple audiences, aggressive creative testing, and enough conversion volume for Meta's algorithm to optimise hard. This is where you start seeing real compounding.

The formula: work backwards from your numbers

Forget rules of thumb. Do this maths:

  1. Customer value: How much is one customer worth to you? (Not one order — the full lifetime value if you have repeat buyers.)
  2. Acceptable cost per customer: If one customer is worth ₹3,000, can you afford to pay ₹750 to acquire them?
  3. Monthly target: 100 customers × ₹750 = ₹75,000/month ad budget.

If the maths doesn't work at your current margins, the answer isn't a bigger budget — it's a better offer, better creative, or a better funnel. That's exactly what we check in a free audit.

Three mistakes that burn small budgets

  • Splitting ₹30,000 across 6 campaigns. Each campaign gets ₹5,000 and none of them learns anything. Run fewer campaigns with meaningful budgets.
  • Optimising for the wrong event. If you sell online, optimise for purchases — not traffic, not engagement. Cheap clicks that don't buy are expensive.
  • Pausing after 3 days. Meta needs roughly 50 conversion events per ad set per week to exit learning. Give a test at least 7–10 days before judging it.

Should you hire an agency at a small budget?

Honestly? If your ad spend is under ₹30,000/month, a full-service agency fee may not make sense — learn the basics yourself first. Above that, a good agency usually pays for itself by cutting wasted spend in the first month. Ask any agency you're considering: "Show me one account you took from under 2x ROAS to above 3x, and tell me what you changed." If they can't answer specifically, keep looking.

The bottom line

Start with ₹30,000–₹50,000/month, one clear offer, and a 30-day test mindset. Track cost per result — not likes, not reach. Scale what works, kill what doesn't, and never let anyone tell you a bigger budget fixes a broken funnel.
