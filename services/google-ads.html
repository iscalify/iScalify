Before we start

Your questions,
answered

Can you review or manage an existing Google Ads account?

Yes. An account review helps identify what to retain and where structure, keywords, ads or tracking need attention. Changes and ongoing management are based on the agreed scope.

Do you manage Search, Shopping and Display?

These campaign types are included in our listed Google Ads service. We recommend the appropriate mix after reviewing the offer, store or website, assets and goals.

What will the reports show?

Weekly reports cover campaign performance and the next actions. Depending on the conversion setup, useful measures include spend, enquiries, purchases and cost per result. Revenue-based measures need reliable purchase data.

Does Google Ads management include SEO?

Google Ads and organic SEO are separate services. Ads involve media spend and campaign management; SEO works on organic discovery. We can discuss either through our services page.

What does it cost?

The management fee is agreed after the audit and depends on scope. Advertising spend is a separate budget. We clarify both before work starts and do not promise a particular return or result date.
