A connected service

From discovery to qualified demand

01

Paid search

Reach people looking for a service you offer. We organise Google Ads around service intent, useful location boundaries and relevant landing pages. Search terms and enquiries inform exclusions and changes, so an emergency repair and a planned installation can have different campaigns and expectations.

02

Meta lead generation

Use Facebook and Instagram to introduce a clear offer and explain why someone should enquire. The form should ask enough to assess fit, such as service, location and timing. We review the quality of the conversation that follows alongside the number of submissions.

03

Local SEO

Make your services, coverage and business details understandable across your website and an eligible Google Business Profile. We review service pages, internal links and real local information. Your profile and website should describe where your business actually operates and how customers can reach it.

04

Landing pages

Give each important offer a page that answers practical questions: what is included, where you work, what happens after an enquiry and what evidence supports your claims. Make the main contact route easy to use on a phone, with a form that fits the buying decision.

05

Measurement

Agree what counts as an enquiry, a qualified lead, a booking and a new customer. Google Ads distinguishes qualified and converted lead goals. Where your systems support it, we can scope reporting that connects campaign activity with those later outcomes.

06

Follow-up planning

Assign an owner to new enquiries and record the reason a lead is accepted, lost or still open. Marketing decisions need that feedback. A working callback process, a clear quote and accurate availability help us understand whether the next improvement belongs in the campaign or the sales process.

Before adding spend

Check the commercial fit

This service suits businesses with a defined offer, realistic delivery capacity and someone responsible for responding to enquiries. Trades, home services, appointment businesses and professional services can have different sales journeys; the plan should reflect those differences. Share your priority service, usual job value, service area and current lead sources before choosing a channel.

Platform spend and agency fees are separate. We agree scope, access, asset responsibilities and reporting before starting. A useful budget is one the business can evaluate against qualified leads and acquired customers. We do not set a universal cost-per-lead target or promise a position in search results.

Buyer questions

Decide what comes first

Should we start with Google Ads, Meta Ads or SEO?

Choose around the buying journey. Search can address an expressed need, social ads can explain an offer, and local SEO can improve how your business is represented. Your existing demand, website and response process help determine the first priority.

Can we start with one service or one location?

Yes. A focused brief can make the offer, routing and lead-quality review easier to manage. Expand when the business has evidence and capacity to support another service or area.
