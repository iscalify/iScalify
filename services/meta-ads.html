Budget and management fees

Your ad budget and our management fee are separate. After the audit, we agree the campaign scope, creative responsibilities and fee. The testing budget should reflect your offer, margins and available conversion data; a larger budget alone cannot fix an unclear offer or a difficult buying journey.

For an online store, reporting needs to connect spend with purchases. For a service business, the conversation also needs lead quality and follow-up outcomes.

Before we start

Your questions,
answered

Can you take over an existing Meta Ads account?

Yes. We start by reviewing the structure, tracking, audiences and creatives. The audit informs what to keep, what to change and the scope of ongoing management.

Is creative production included?

Creative direction and ad copywriting are part of our listed service. The assets needed, who produces them and any additional production scope are agreed before work begins.

How much should we spend?

We discuss your customer value, margins, goals and current data before recommending a testing budget. Platform spend is separate from the management fee; both are clarified after the audit.

How quickly will we know what is working?

It depends on the budget, offer, conversion volume and tracking quality. We review performance weekly and explain what the available data supports, without promising a fixed ROAS or deadline.
