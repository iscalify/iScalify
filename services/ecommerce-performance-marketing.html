Before investing

A sensible starting point

  • Ecommerce businesses with a working checkout, reliable inventory and an understanding of product margins, not only an advertising account.
  • Brands exploring which part of the purchase journey needs improvement: discovery, consideration, product selection, checkout or repeat orders.
  • Teams that can grant appropriate advertising and analytics access and share performance context needed for a useful review.

Budget and scope

The starting channel mix and test budget depend on product price, demand, fulfilment capacity and customer value. Platform ad spend and campaign management are separately agreed. We do not claim that a fixed advertising budget produces a fixed sales outcome.

Campaign accounts and customer data remain under the client's control, with appropriate permissions and agreed reporting responsibilities.

Questions to ask

What to clarify first

Should we start with Google Ads or Meta Ads?

It depends on whether customers already search for the category, product-feed readiness, creative strength and the cost of acquisition your margins can support.

Is Shopify required?

No. The commercial approach can apply to other ecommerce platforms, but the implementation details depend on the store's setup.

Do you report on blended results?

Where the store shares reliable order and advertising data, we can discuss total acquisition outcomes alongside platform metrics, noting attribution limitations.

Can we start with one channel?

Yes. A focused experiment is often preferable to spreading a small budget across channels without enough conversion data.
