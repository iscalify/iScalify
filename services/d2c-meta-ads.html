What we review

A purchase-first Meta Ads plan

01

Offer and unit economics

Before changing bids or audiences, we review price, gross margin, shipping, returns and the first-order versus returning-customer story. Ad account ROAS alone is not the same as profit.

02

Creative testing

Map customer objections to distinct messages, formats and proof points. Agree who supplies product footage, reviews and landing assets before planning a test.

03

Account structure

Keep prospecting and retention intent understandable, avoid unnecessary audience fragmentation and align campaign decisions with conversion volume.

04

Purchase measurement

Review pixel and event setup, attribution limitations, checkout events and discrepancies between the store and the platform. Do not present platform figures as audited revenue.

05

Product page conversion

Inspect mobile speed, size information, delivery terms, trust signals and checkout friction before scaling paid traffic.

06

Decision reporting

Compare spend, purchases, revenue, cost per new customer and contribution margin where data supports it; document what the numbers cannot establish.

Before investing

Who this service fits

  • An established or launching D2C brand with products customers can buy online, a functioning checkout and the ability to fulfil orders in its target market.
  • Brands that can provide authorised access to their advertising account, storefront and suitable tracking, or are prepared to resolve measurement gaps before investing heavily.
  • A business willing to evaluate the full acquisition path, including refunds, repeat orders and its commercial constraints rather than asking for a guaranteed ROAS.

Budget and scope

Meta spend is separate from agency management fees and any creative-production costs. A suitable testing budget depends on customer value, margins, available creative and expected purchase volume. We agree access, creative responsibilities, timeline and scope before work begins.

Campaign accounts and customer data remain under the client's control, with appropriate permissions and agreed reporting responsibilities.

Working with Iscalify

A practical four-step process

  1. 01

    Audit the purchase journey

    Review store funnel, conversion events, product economics, current creatives and historical campaign decisions.

  2. 02

    Choose the first test

    Agree a primary product group, geography, creative hypothesis, spending limits and measurement definitions.

  3. 03

    Launch and check

    Verify live ad destinations, checkout experience and events, then compare early outcomes with the hypothesis.

  4. 04

    Review and refine

    Use purchase data and business context to adjust the next test, not a fixed promised performance target.

Questions to ask

What to clarify first

Can you work with a US or UK brand from India?

Yes, this is planned as remote delivery from Mohali. Meeting times, approvals, account permissions and local advertising context should be agreed before a project starts.

Does the service include creating every ad asset?

Creative strategy and ad copy can be scoped, while product photography, editing, UGC permissions and production responsibilities are agreed separately.

Do you guarantee a specific return on ad spend?

No. Results depend on demand, offer, margin, tracking, creative and conversion performance. We establish measurable goals and discuss uncertainty.

How do you distinguish platform ROAS from profit?

We compare ad reporting with store data and consider cost of goods, shipping, returns and fees where the client can share reliable figures.
