CASE STUDY 01 · META ADS · INDIA

FASHION & APPAREL —
2.3 → 5.6 ROAS · +136% REVENUE

The Strategy & Execution

The account was running at around 2.3 blended ROAS with spend spread across 45 campaigns. We switched off everything that wasn't converting, rebuilt the account into a simple prospecting and retargeting structure, and re-cut the brand's existing videos with new hooks instead of producing new creatives.

First month after the restructure: blended ROAS reached 5.6 with top campaigns between 5.9 and 7.0. Store revenue jumped +136% and monthly orders doubled.
