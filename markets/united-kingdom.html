Market planning

Connect local visibility with real coverage

Describe the towns, postcodes and types of property your business serves before adding location pages or expanding campaigns. A county-wide description may hide important differences in travel time, job size and availability. A useful area page should explain something specific about your service there, supported by real operations or work you can document.

For an eligible service-area business, Google Business Profile uses named areas such as towns or postal codes rather than a radius. If customers are not served at your business address, Google says that address should be removed from the public profile. We use the official service-area guidance to review your profile against how your business operates.

We would not create a local office claim to make a page appear nearby. Your UK business information belongs on its own website and profile; Iscalify’s delivery location remains Mohali, India. Keep contact details, opening hours and the services listed consistent with the customer’s actual experience.

Campaigns and customer experience

Give each enquiry a clear next step

Choose whether the offer is a callback, a survey, an estimate or a bookable appointment. Those actions need different information. A property-maintenance enquiry may need a postcode, property type and description of the job; a consultation may need a suitable date and a short explanation of the problem.

For paid search, align the advert and landing page with the service being requested. Separate an urgent repair from a planned installation where their economics and response requirements differ. Keep the terms familiar to your audience, and show any agreed prices or estimates in pounds with enough context to understand what is included.

Meta campaigns can support services that benefit from a visual explanation or a considered decision. Ask for the details needed to qualify the request, then send it to someone who can respond. A lower form-submission cost is not useful if the enquiry falls outside your coverage or the customer expected a different service.

Working together

Make the review about accepted work

Connect website forms and calls to a simple record of qualified enquiries, estimates sent, jobs accepted and reasons for loss. Compare channels on the same definition. A small local SEO improvement, a clearer service page or a change to callback handling may be the next practical action; the review should explain why.

Our remote process starts with your coverage, services, website and available performance data. We agree access, an approval contact and a meeting window that works for UK operating hours. Your business owns its accounts and confirms the accuracy of service claims, photographs and qualifications before publication.

Before we start

Questions for your marketing partner

Can you manage our Google Business Profile from India?

We can include an authorised profile review and agreed updates in the scope. Your business must meet Google’s eligibility requirements, retain ownership and provide accurate information about its real operations.

Do we need a separate page for every town?

Only when each page helps a customer understand a genuinely distinct service offering or coverage detail. Repeating the same text with different town names gives people little reason to read it.
