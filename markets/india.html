Market planning

Match the city, service and conversation

Start with the service you want to sell and the locations where you can fulfil it. A business serving Mohali, Chandigarh and Panchkula should define its real coverage and travel limits rather than treating every enquiry from the wider region as suitable. Use the same approach for the cities and neighbourhoods where your business operates.

Choose the languages you can support across the advert, landing page and follow-up. English, Hindi, Punjabi or another regional language may belong in the brief depending on your customers and team. Review the wording with someone who understands the service and the audience; a translated headline alone cannot carry a consultation or quotation.

Google’s current language guidance explains that language signals and ad language affect matching, with Search changes beginning in September 2026. We check account settings and actual enquiries rather than assuming a search language equals the customer’s preferred conversation.

Campaigns and customer experience

Choose a channel with a clear job

Google Ads can focus on people searching for a specific service. Separate an urgent repair from a considered consultation or a recurring service when the customer’s next step differs. Build the landing page around the offer, location, useful evidence and one clear contact action. Confirm the team can answer during the advertised hours.

Meta lead generation can introduce an offer to people who need more explanation before enquiring. Ask for details that determine fit, such as service, city and preferred timing. A campaign with inexpensive submissions can still be difficult to use if the contact details are incomplete or the person expected a service you do not provide.

For local SEO, keep the website and an eligible Google Business Profile accurate. Google’s service-area guidance distinguishes businesses that visit customers from those that also serve them at a business address. We review the setup against real operations instead of creating city pages or profiles that imply additional offices.

Working together

Turn calls and messages into usable feedback

If your business uses WhatsApp, agree who answers, which questions qualify a request and where the outcome is recorded. A website click on a phone or WhatsApp link is an interaction; it does not by itself establish a qualified lead or booked appointment. Forms, calls and messages need consistent definitions in the report.

Keep a simple record of contact attempts, suitable enquiries, appointments, quotes and paying customers. Compare spend with customer value in rupees, with agency fees and platform budget stated separately. Lead quality and response capacity should guide the next decision about targeting, page content or budget.

We work from Mohali and agree the service scope, approval contact and review schedule before starting. You supply accurate service details, available capacity and approved business evidence. We use that information to plan and review campaigns while your team handles customer advice, pricing and delivery.

Before we start

Questions for your marketing partner

Can we begin with one city and one offer?

Yes. A defined location and service make it easier to agree the landing page, enquiry questions and follow-up responsibilities before considering a wider campaign.

Is there one budget that works for every service?

No. The starting scope depends on the service, margins, market, available demand and what can be measured. We discuss a testing budget separately from management fees and avoid promising a fixed lead cost.
