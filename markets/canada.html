Market planning

Start with a serviceable region

Build the brief around accepted cities and postal areas, rather than treating Canada as one service territory. Ask which jobs your team can reach, which locations carry a different call-out cost and whether residential and commercial work use different crews. Put those distinctions into your targeting review and enquiry questions.

Decide which customer languages your business can support from advert through quotation. If you want English and French enquiries, both journeys need appropriate copy, a usable landing page and someone able to continue the conversation. Translation alone does not establish that follow-up capacity; agree who will review each version before publication.

Google’s current language-targeting guidance describes how ad language and language signals influence matching, including changes to Search campaigns beginning in September 2026. We review the account’s current controls and actual search terms instead of assuming a language setting isolates every enquiry.

Campaigns and customer experience

Design the offer around the booking decision

For a home-service enquiry, ask for the service needed, location and timing before adding questions that slow the form down. A recurring cleaning arrangement, an emergency repair and a renovation estimate have different qualification needs. Give each important service a clear explanation of the process and what the customer should expect next.

If demand or staff capacity changes through the year, use your own booking history and operating calendar to plan campaign emphasis. An available appointment or crew is more useful than a broad seasonal assumption. Explain temporary coverage changes on the landing page and update the people answering calls.

Google Ads can address active searches, while Meta can introduce a considered offer using genuine project images and an explanation of the service. Review local SEO alongside paid campaigns so the business details and services a customer finds remain accurate. Keep budgets and customer-value reporting in an agreed currency, such as Canadian dollars, to make comparisons understandable.

Working together

Follow the lead through to accepted work

Record the customer’s service request, postal area, preferred language, contact outcome and booking status. Compare qualified enquiries separately from duplicate forms, employment requests and locations your team cannot serve. When customers take time to decide, keep open opportunities visible so a weekly report does not confuse an unfinished quote with a lost job.

Our team works from Mohali, India. Agree meeting times in the Canadian time zone relevant to your business, a contact for approvals and how lead feedback will be shared. We can scope campaign and website work; your team supplies accurate local information, confirms language quality and handles customer conversations.

Before we start

Questions for your marketing partner

Can we launch in one city before expanding?

Yes. Choose a service and region your team can support, then use enquiry quality, booked work and available capacity to decide whether another region belongs in the plan.

Will every campaign need French content?

The content should match the audience you intend to serve and the languages your business can support. We agree that scope with you rather than assuming the same language journey for every Canadian region.
