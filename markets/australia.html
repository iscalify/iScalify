Market planning

Use the coverage your team recognises

List the suburbs and postcodes your team accepts, including areas with different travel or call-out requirements. A map radius may cross a practical boundary your crew would not treat as nearby. Review the geographic targets available in the account against your operating map, then collect enough location information to check the enquiries that arrive.

Google notes that location target types vary by country. We verify the selectable areas rather than promising every suburb or postcode will be an independent target. Where the account’s map does not match your business boundary precisely, the landing page and qualification process still need to make coverage clear.

Separate a call-out, an on-site quote and a larger scheduled project when they need different staff or have different margins. For example, a small electrical repair and a planned switchboard upgrade may deserve different messages and forms. Use your own service names and quote history to decide the structure.

Campaigns and customer experience

Make the path to a quote easy to follow

A search landing page should explain the work, accepted area, contact hours and what happens after a request. If you need photographs or a site visit before pricing, say so. Show only credentials and project details the business can substantiate. Confirm any stated prices and inclusions before an advert sends customers to them.

For Meta, an offer might introduce a planned service with real project photographs, a useful explanation and a clear enquiry action. Ask for the suburb and type of work where those details determine fit. Review the completed conversations rather than judging the campaign only by the cost of a form submission.

Local SEO should describe actual services and operations. A service-area business’s Google Business Profile is different from an advertising radius: Google uses named areas and provides specific service-area guidance. We can review that setup within the agreed scope without inventing extra storefronts or local addresses.

Working together

Keep marketing connected to the work calendar

Track enquiries through contacted, suitable, quoted and booked stages. Note why a job was declined, including location, job type or unavailable dates. If quote capacity is full, that should inform campaign decisions before more spend is added. Review customer acquisition costs in a consistent reporting currency, usually Australian dollars when that is how the business budgets.

Delivery is from Mohali, India. We agree review times in your state’s local time, who approves adverts and who updates us on crew availability. Your business retains account ownership and manages calls, site visits and quotes. Shared feedback lets the next campaign change respond to what is happening in the business.

Before we start

Questions for your marketing partner

Can you market a business that has no shopfront?

Yes, the website and campaigns can explain a genuine service area. Any Google Business Profile work must follow the eligibility and address rules that apply to your business model.

Should we advertise every service at once?

Start with the work you want, can quote reliably and have capacity to deliver. Add another service when its offer, landing page and response process are ready to be evaluated.
