Market planning

Define the area your team can serve

Start with the actual ZIP codes, cities and travel boundaries your team accepts. For example, a home-service operator may cover several suburbs but decline work across a bridge or outside a technician’s route. Use those operating limits when reviewing campaign locations, exclusions and the address information requested in the enquiry form.

Google Ads can reach people who are present in a location or show interest in it. For a business that requires an on-site visit, we review whether presence-based targeting fits the brief, then examine the locations of real enquiries. Google’s location guidance also makes clear that targeting is not perfectly accurate.

Separate urgent work from considered purchases. “AC repair” and a planned HVAC replacement need different questions, response expectations and landing-page explanations. Use the terms your customers and dispatch team use. Avoid an emergency message when no one is available to answer or send a technician.

Campaigns and customer experience

Choose the route to a qualified appointment

Paid search can focus on a defined service and area. Meta campaigns may suit planned projects where photographs, an explanation of the process or a clear estimate offer help a customer decide. Keep residential and commercial enquiries distinguishable if your pricing, crew or sales process differs.

For eligible categories, Google Local Services Ads may be another option to evaluate alongside standard search campaigns. Availability depends on the business category and area, so we would check the official eligibility information before including it in a proposal. Eligibility is not an assumption about your business.

Use a landing page that states the service, accepted area, contact hours and next step. Include only genuine project photographs, verified credentials and customer feedback you have permission to publish. A click-to-call action should lead to the team responsible for that market, with an alternative enquiry route when the phone is unattended.

Working together

Review calls against booked jobs

Ask your team to record service requested, location, whether the call was answered, booking outcome and job value when known. Distinguish an existing customer, a job seeker and an out-of-area enquiry from a new prospect. Agree whether the acquisition calculation includes ad spend alone or also agency and other marketing costs.

We deliver the work remotely from Mohali, India. Before launch, agree review times in your local time zone, approval responsibilities and who handles urgent campaign requests. You retain ownership of your accounts; your team remains responsible for customer calls, quotes and service delivery.
